Six rules the product is held to.

V1Prove it, do not promise itIf we cannot show the mechanism, we do not make the claim.
V2The engineer is the protagonistMore capability for the person accountable. Never more autonomy for the model.
V3Name the limit firstDegraded is printed in amber, not hidden.
V4Files, not conversationsThe next session reads the decision instead of re-making it.
V5Your code stays yoursNo telemetry, no account, no server.
V6Open to inspectionEvery installed file carries its origin and hash.
