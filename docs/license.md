# The licence, in plain English

EDIFY's public edition is released under the **[MIT License](../LICENSE)**. It is
short, and it is worth the two minutes — but here is the summary, and the summary is
binding on nothing. The [LICENSE](../LICENSE) is the licence.

## The short version

You may use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and sell copies of
EDIFY, at work or anywhere else, on any code, at any company size, as long as the
copyright notice and the licence text travel with it. There is no warranty.

That is the whole of it. There is no non-compete clause, no delayed conversion, and
no field-of-use restriction.

## The name is separate

The licence covers the code. The name "EDIFY", the logo and the `edify` command name
are trademarks, covered by [TRADEMARK.md](../TRADEMARK.md): fork freely, and rename
before you ship something of your own.

## The team edition

The team edition, `edify-agents-teams`, is issued by licence on request. A team or
partner licence is a signed token verified locally, with no phone-home. There is no
payment and no price.
